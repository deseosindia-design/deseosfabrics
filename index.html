Our Story

Born From A Passion For Beautiful Fabric

Deseos — a Spanish word for "Wishes" and "Desires." Our genie lamp carries that spirit — at Deseos Fabrics, we believe every fabric holds a wish waiting to become something extraordinary.

Deseos Fabrics was founded with one belief — that the right fabric transforms not just an outfit, but the confidence of the person wearing it. We are a Mumbai-based premium fabric sourcing and curation brand, operating exclusively online.

We curate from the finest sources across India and beyond — handwoven silks, artisan chanderi, luxurious georgettes, imported fabrics from global markets, specialised printed textiles, and exclusive weaves you won't find just anywhere — brought directly to you, whether you're a home stitcher, a boutique owner, or a fashion designer building a collection.

We don't just sell fabric. We help you design confidence.

Built on a passion for fabric, trusted by thousands across India and the world — and always, warmly welcoming everyone. Whether you're a fashion label or someone who simply fell in love with a beautiful print, you are always welcome here.

Every fabric in our collection is handpicked and quality-checked. We source from the finest mills, artisans, and global suppliers — so you always receive the best, curated with care.
